ecomgen.io

EcomGen

The AI studio that builds your fashion brand.

EcomGen is an AI fashion production studio for fashion, beauty and lifestyle brands. Upload your product photos and it produces on-model photos, catalogue images, lifestyle visuals, short videos and ad creatives, with no prompt writing needed. Brand DNA keeps your colours, style and tone consistent across everything it makes.

Visit EcomGen →

What you can do

  • Generate on-model, studio, flat-lay and marketplace product photos
  • Create product videos, reels and campaign videos
  • Swap the cast and outfits in a reference video with Swapped
  • Save your Brand DNA so every generation follows your style
  • Plan and run full campaigns with Campaign Builder
  • Process up to 50 products in one batch

Built for fashion, jewellery, beauty and lifestyle brands, D2C sellers, agencies and creative studios. Works with Amazon, Myntra, Flipkart, Meesho, ASOS, Ajio, Shopify and WooCommerce. 250 free credits to start, no card needed.

cloudface-ai.com

CloudFace AI

Every guest finds their event photos with one selfie.

CloudFace AI is an AI face search platform for event photos. Organisers and photographers upload an event's photos and share a QR code or WhatsApp link. Guests take a single selfie, see every photo they appear in, and download them all in one ZIP. Guests do not need to install an app.

Visit CloudFace AI →

What it does

  • Selfie face search that works on group, angled and low-light shots
  • Upload from a device or Google Drive, or live from a camera with the desktop uploader for Windows and Mac
  • Share each event with a QR code or a WhatsApp link
  • Custom branding, logo and watermark on deliveries
  • One-click ZIP download in original quality
  • Encrypted storage (AES-256) and one-click data deletion

Built for photographers, event organisers, wedding studios and corporate teams. Start free with 1,000 photos for 7 days, no card needed.

trimpixo.com

TrimPixo

Simple online image tools.

TrimPixo is a set of online image tools. Its batch watermark tool adds a watermark to many images in one go and returns them as a single ZIP, so branding a full set of pictures takes a few clicks.

Visit TrimPixo →

What it does

  • Batch watermark: apply one watermark to many images at once
  • Works with JPG, PNG and WEBP files
  • Choose from six placement positions
  • Download all results as one ZIP
  • Free to use

Built for anyone who needs to watermark or brand images in bulk.
